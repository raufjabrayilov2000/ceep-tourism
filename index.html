Our Origin

Born From
CEEP.

Seven years of European programs — now opening the world.

CEEP Tourism is the premium travel arm of the Center for European Education Programs — an organization that has spent seven years guiding ambitious individuals through Europe's most storied cities. From Prague to Barcelona, we built not just programs, but experiences that stayed with people.

Now we bring that same obsessive care, insider network, and European refinement to leisure travel. Whether you seek a private villa in Santorini, a dawn safari in Cape Town, or a ryokan stay in Kyoto — we design journeys that feel personal, not packaged.

We do not sell tours. We craft itineraries shaped around you.
